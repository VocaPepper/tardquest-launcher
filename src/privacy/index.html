This privacy policy describes how TardQuest (single-player) and TardQuest Online II (multiplayer) — together "the Services" — collect, use, and handle your data. Each product is covered separately below. Sections 1–10 apply to the original TardQuest. Section 11 covers TardQuest Online II.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Session Data

When you play, the Service creates an anonymous session to track your game progress. This includes:

  • A randomly generated session ID (not linked to any personal identity)
  • Your game progress (floor, level, experience points)
  • Your in-game inventory (e.g. carrier pigeons)
  • Timestamps related to your session activity

No account, email address, or password is required or collected.

1.2 Leaderboard Submissions

If you choose to submit a leaderboard entry, we store:

  • Your chosen display name (up to 5 characters)
  • Your floor and level at the time of submission

Leaderboard submissions require completing a CAPTCHA challenge (see Section 3).

1.3 Pigeon Messages

The in-game carrier pigeon system lets you send short anonymous messages to other players. We store the message text and basic game info about the sender (floor, level) for delivery purposes. Messages are anonymous — no personal identity is attached.

1.4 Server Logs

We keep standard server logs that include your IP address, the pages you access, and your browser type. These logs are used to keep the Service running smoothly and to detect abuse. Logs are automatically rotated — older entries are regularly overwritten.

1.5 Anti-Cheat and Abuse Prevention

To keep the game fair, we monitor gameplay patterns to detect cheating and automated abuse. If suspicious activity is detected from your IP address, it may be temporarily blocked. These blocks expire automatically within 1 hour.

2. Data Retention

  • Game sessions expire after 2 hours of inactivity and are permanently deleted after 7 days.
  • Leaderboard entries are stored indefinitely.
  • Pigeon messages are stored indefinitely.
  • Server logs are rotated regularly; older entries are automatically overwritten.
  • Temporary IP blocks expire after 1 hour.

3. Third-Party Services

Cloudflare Turnstile

Leaderboard submissions use Cloudflare Turnstile for CAPTCHA verification. When you complete a CAPTCHA, your response and IP address are sent to Cloudflare. This is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

The following applies to the original TardQuest. TardQuest Online II does collect certain personal data — see Section 11.

  • No personal accounts, emails, or passwords
  • No cookies or browser fingerprinting
  • No device identifiers or hardware information
  • No precise location data
  • No payment or financial information
  • No third-party analytics or advertising trackers

5. How Data Is Used

All collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Running the game — saving your progress, leaderboard rankings, and in-game messaging
  • Fair play — detecting and preventing cheating and abuse
  • Keeping the Service online — diagnosing errors and maintaining reliability

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including secure session generation, input sanitization, and safe database practices. No personal identifying information is stored beyond IP addresses in server logs.

7. Data Access

Your IP address and session data are never exposed to other players. Administrative tools are restricted to authorized access only.

8. Your Rights

Since the Service does not require accounts or collect personal information, there are no user profiles to access, correct, or delete. All sessions are anonymous and expire automatically.

If your IP address is temporarily blocked, the block will lift automatically within 1 hour.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Service. The "Last Updated" date at the bottom will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, reach out via email at vocapepper@outlook.com

11. TardQuest Online II — Additional Data Practices

The following applies to TardQuest Online II, a separate multiplayer product with account-based progression. This section supplements Sections 1–10 with practices specific to TardQuest Online II.

11.1 Account Registration

To play TardQuest Online II, you must register an account. We collect and store:

  • Username — a unique name you choose, visible to other players in-game
  • Password — stored as a salted cryptographic hash. The plaintext password is never accessible to us
  • Account metadata — account creation timestamp, last login timestamp, and last active timestamp

11.2 Persistent Game Progress

Your game state is saved to our database and loaded when you log in. This includes:

  • Your current floor, position, and zone
  • Combat stats (HP, defense, strength, persuasion, endurance, speed, luck)
  • Level, experience points, total experience, and prestige level
  • Bitcoins (in-game currency)
  • Inventory, equipped weapons/armor/rings, and item enhancement data
  • Checkpoint (respawn) position
  • Quest progress (kill counts, claimed quests)
  • Companion data and party membership

11.3 Chat Messages

TardQuest Online II includes a persistent in-game chat system. Chat messages are stored and include:

  • Your username (as sender)
  • The message text
  • The chat scope (global, party, or whisper) and recipient (for whispers)
  • The floor and instance where the message was sent
  • A timestamp

11.4 Social Features

Friend requests and friendships are stored in our database to enable social features. This includes the usernames of both parties and the relationship status (pending or accepted).

11.5 Server Logs

Standard server logs include your IP address, the endpoints you access, and your browser type — the same as described in Section 1.4. These are used for service reliability and abuse detection.

11.6 Anti-Cheat

Gameplay patterns are monitored to detect cheating and automated abuse, as described in Section 1.5.

11.7 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while the account is active. You may request account deletion via the contact channels in Section 10.
  • Chat messages are stored indefinitely for message history.
  • Server logs are rotated regularly; older entries are automatically overwritten.
  • Temporary IP blocks expire after 1 hour.

11.8 Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by reaching out through the channels listed in Section 10. Account deletion removes your username, game progress, and chat messages from our systems.

11.9 What We Do NOT Collect (Both Products)

  • No cookies or browser fingerprinting
  • No device identifiers or hardware information
  • No precise location data
  • No payment or financial information
  • No third-party analytics or advertising trackers

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling.
